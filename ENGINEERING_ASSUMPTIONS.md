# Engineering assumption and evidence register

Updated: 2026-09-24. This register separates geometry that can be checked mathematically from product requirements that still need a specific manual, site data, and independent review. The app is a planning tool; it does not issue construction approval.

| Model area | Current basis | Evidence status | Required before construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall and cap unit counts | User-entered unit dimensions and joints; centerline perimeter divided by module length, then course rules | Deterministic geometry with unit tests. Real unit tolerances, cuts, and bond details have not been field-validated. | Dry-lay a course, measure actual stock and joint widths, and reconcile cut and purchase counts. |
| Circular and polygon perimeter | Circle `πD`; regular hexagon/octagon `n × span × tan(π/n)` for across-flats span | Mathematical identity. Polygon stone face area now uses the same outer perimeter as the geometry engine. | Confirm that the chosen masonry footprint follows the modeled regular shape. |
| Natural-stone quantity | Exposed outer face area; illustrative 4 in and 8 in depth coverage at 40 and 20 ft²/ton | Model assumption, not a supplier coverage rate. Weight range is now derived from those same tonnage estimates. | Use the exact stone supplier's coverage and unit weight; measure wall thickness and waste allowance. |
| Foundation base | 8 in angular stone and 6 in extension per side | Fixed quantity assumption; no soil-bearing, drainage, frost-footing, or structural design verification. | Obtain site-specific foundation/footing detail for soil, slope, runoff, frost, and applicable local rules. |
| Horizontal clearance | [USFA general fire-pit advice](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/outdoor-fires/) of at least 10 ft from combustibles | General public safety guidance, not jurisdictional approval. A [TIKI Patio product page](https://tikibrand.com/collections/smokeless-fire-pits/products/patio-smokeless-fire-pit) specifies 15 ft for that product. | Check the actual appliance manual, local fire authority, overhead conditions, and any stricter setback. |
| Overhead clearance | User-entered vertical spacing to combustibles | The former unsourced 21 ft wood / 15 ft gas markers have been removed. The app now leaves this as an unresolved review item. | Use the product instructions and site-specific authority guidance; inspect all overhead combustibles. |
| Gas enclosure venting | Generic 18–60 in² category bands, with modeled total geometric opening | Illustrative only. No category identifies a manufacturer SKU. [American Fire Glass](https://americanfireglass.com/media/manual/Match%20Light%20Kits.pdf) instructs two opposing vents of 18 in² **each** for a cited propane kit; [The Outdoor Plus](https://theoutdoorplus.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Fire-Pit-Manual.pdf) gives a BTU-based rule for its products. | Obtain the exact burner, pan, valve, and enclosure manuals. Verify *free* area per required side, vent position, crossflow, unobstructed paths, gas routing, and installer sign-off. |
| Double-wall firebox materials | Generic firebrick/stone/brick categories, selected mortar, and cavity dimensions | Product-specific temperature ratings were removed because preset names alone do not establish them. | Record unit and mortar SKU, datasheet heat rating, installation method, expansion and moisture details, and assembly review. |
| Mortar drying and first fire | Selected product instructions | There is no universal 28-day schedule. [RUTLAND castable cement](https://rutland.com/products/castable-refractory-cement) and [QUIKRETE fireplace mortar](https://www.quikrete.com/PDFs/DATA_SHEET-Fireplace-Mortar-8620-21.pdf) have different procedures. | Use the exact selected product's cure and staged heat-up instructions. |
| DIY smokeless liner | User dimensions; area ratio band 1.2–1.5; stack-pressure formula with assumed air temperatures | Illustrative, **not** validated airflow, combustion, fabrication, or support engineering. The calculated hole pattern is a review sketch. | Independent thermal/airflow and structural review, prototype testing, and material and joining specifications before fabrication or use. |
| Branded smokeless pits | Earlier versions offered Solo Stove, Breeo, and TIKI fit profiles | Those profiles mixed fire-pit body dimensions with invented flange/air-gap data. New selection is disabled and legacy selections generate no fit geometry. [Breeo's actual insert-ring page](https://breeo.com/products/x-series-insert-ring) specifies surround opening **ranges** of 22–26, 27–33, and 34–39 in for X19/X24/X30, each at least 15 in deep. | Use an exact manufacturer-approved surround system and current manual; do not convert a freestanding product's outer diameter into a flange specification. |
| Natural-stone heat behavior | Stone category selection | No stone name alone certifies direct-flame suitability. The [Smithsonian stone weathering review](https://repository.si.edu/bitstreams/f3b86787-fc58-4295-89c6-193ee34e13e3/download) discusses heat damage and spalling, including granite. | Verify the actual stone and heat exposure, specify a heat-rated inner assembly, and review drainage and thermal movement. |

## Release gates for a construction-ready claim

1. Select exact products and gather current installation manuals and material datasheets. Replace generic gas, liner, mortar, and material assumptions with traceable product requirements.
2. Validate geometry and bill-of-material results against measured dry-lay examples for every supported plan shape, including cuts, corners, and purchased waste.
3. Obtain site-specific foundation, clearance, venting, gas, and firebox reviews from appropriately qualified professionals and the local authority where required.
4. Add verified fixture cases from those reviews to automated tests. Keep model estimates and approvals separate in the UI and exports.
5. Measure mobile performance and offline update behavior with two deployed versions; inspect the new 192 px and 512 px app icons after installation; review remaining development dependency upgrades before publishing a release candidate.
